Discover the Ocean Through Sound

The ocean is full of noise, from whale songs and fish choruses to waves, wind, and human activity. Riptide Remix lets you explore, layer, and manipulate these recordings to hear the underwater world as marine life does.

Experienced Mixer

Welcome to Riptide Remix!

Thank you for visiting our site. Here, you can explore the fascinating world of oceanic sound and discover how marine life experiences the underwater world every day. Our team is passionate about the ocean and all its acoustic properties, and we hope you will be too.

Our friend Clippy is excited to guide you through the science behind the sounds you’re exploring! Throughout the site, you’ll see Clippy next to items you can learn more about. Click him for detailed explanations and helpful tips.

New Acoustician

If you're new to bioacoustics, start on the "Learn More" page. Explore sound options, listen to samples, and get a quick introduction to why scientists focus on each species.

Click Clippy to go to the Learning Module now. Once you’re familiar with the sounds, select “Mix Sounds” in the menu to start layering them together.

Experienced Scientist

If you're comfortable with bioacoustics, start here! Adjust the acoustic properties of your sounds and explore how they interact. Learn more about the ocean’s soundscape or just have fun creating your own compositions.

Click Clippy to go to the Mixing Module now.
